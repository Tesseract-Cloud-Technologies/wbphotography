02 / About

Behind the Camera

I am Wayne Bruce, a freelance photographer working out of Port Moresby. I started out shooting weddings for friends and family and it grew into a full-time practice covering portraits, events, and editorial work for local businesses.

My approach stays simple. Good light, an honest moment, and as little direction as possible. I want the people in the frame to look like themselves, not like they are posing for a stranger.
